Welcome to the UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive. For over half a century, the UCLA Phonetics Laboratory has collected recordings of languages from around the globe, providing source materials for phonetic and phonological research. On this website, we hope to make our materials more easily accessible than ever before, serving the interests of scholars, speakers, and language learners everywhere.

While our collections are open to everyone interested, our materials are primarily intended to be used by the linguistics community. Our audio material, together with its supplemental text, is comprised mainly of recordings of word lists intended to illustrate the phonetic structures of languages.
